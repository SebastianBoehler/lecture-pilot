# Paper ledger for the LecturePilot evidence audit

This is the compact trace from each of the 25 locally archived full texts to the [implementation audit](2026-09-25-lecturepilot-evidence-audit.md). It records the outcome each source can speak to and the inference it cannot support. Source links and copy types are in the [archive index](2026-09-25-local-fulltext-archive-index.md); detailed methods and access limits are in the [content notes](2026-09-25-paper-content-and-access-notes.md) and linked earlier research notes. The two Tübingen items are article chapters within dissertations. No row is an effect estimate for LecturePilot.

## Retrieval, instruction and self-regulation

| Paper | Relevant finding and limit | Product inference |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Adesope et al. 2017 | Practice-testing meta-analysis favors retrieval on average; high heterogeneity and mostly laboratory outcomes. | Keep genuine retrieval opportunities; assess outcomes in the product. |
| Agarwal et al. 2021 | Systematic review finds classroom retrieval benefits across many applied studies; tasks and delays vary. | Make returning to course material normal, not a one-off end quiz. |
| Atkinson et al. 2003 | Faded worked examples and principle prompts improved near/far transfer in structured probability problems. | Use selective worked-step fading and explanation of principles; do not force it for every subject. |
| Cepeda et al. 2006 | Distributed-practice synthesis: spacing benefit depends on the final retention interval. | Schedule later reviews but avoid claiming an optimal universal delay. |
| Panadero 2017 | SRL model review identifies planning, monitoring/control and appraisal; conceptual synthesis, not app trial. | Connect a learner goal, a feasible plan, progress and post-task adjustment. |
| Martins van Jaarsveld et al. 2025 | Five-week agent goal-monitoring experiment raised goal attainment, not measured academic performance. | Try visible progress against a plan; do not sell calendar sync as a proven grade intervention. |
| Wagner et al. 2024 | Three experiments: elaborated actionable feedback plus instruction improved transfer where brief corrective feedback did not reliably help. | Audit hint/feedback quality and the next learner action, not feedback quantity. |

## Generative AI in teaching

| Paper | Relevant finding and limit | Product inference |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bastani et al. 2025 | In one high-school math trial, unrestricted AI raised assisted practice but hurt immediate unaided performance; guarded tutor mitigated harm, not proven gain. | Preserve help exposure and fresh unaided checks; keep the product-efficacy claim open. |
| Tutor CoPilot / Wang et al. 2025 | Human tutors with AI suggestions improved session exit-ticket topic mastery in a K–12 trial. | Professor/human control is plausible; result does not establish autonomous university tutoring or delayed transfer. |
| LearnLM Team 2025 | Supervised AI drafts in five UK schools were edited by human tutors; exploratory next-topic estimate versus human tutoring was uncertain. | Evaluate feedback and pacing with human review; do not generalize to autonomous efficacy. |

## Instructional visuals and navigation

| Paper | Relevant finding and limit | Product inference |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Berney & Bétrancourt 2016 | Animation/static meta-analysis has small mean animation advantage and substantial heterogeneity. | Select motion only when the target needs motion; test the matching learning outcome. |
| Ploetzner et al. 2021 | Animation supported motion identification; a static display better supported spatial arrangements in one linkage task. | Choose the representation for the operation, not for visual appeal. |
| Porta Simó et al. 2015 | Course map raised optional activity completion without a significant posttest-grade effect. | Clear navigation may aid participation; measure learning separately. |
| Stoesz et al. 2020 | LMS screenshots' visual metrics tracked perceived complexity in small lab studies; learning was unmeasured. | Keep the canvas readable, but do not treat visual simplicity as proven learning gain. |
| Russ et al. 2026 | Classroom physics experiment: no significant overall immediate or eight-week conceptual gain from generation, retrieval or their sequence; quality moderation exploratory. | A mixed-question sequence needs a goal-specific reason and a controlled outcome test. |

## Memory mechanisms, transfer and attention

| Paper | Relevant finding and limit | Product inference |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Brod et al. 2022 | Prediction especially supported unexpected numerical facts; small groups and narrow tasks. | Use occasional predictions when the discrepancy teaches the concept. |
| Denis et al. 2026 | Encoding theta related to sleep-dependent memory processes in a small nap study; neural associations are not a learning-app prescription. | Preserve delayed outcomes; no brain-based schedule or sleep monitoring claim. |
| Fisher et al. 2015 | Searching for explanations inflated self-rated internal knowledge in online experiments. | Show supported versus independent evidence; confidence alone is insufficient. |
| Kontra et al. 2015 | Manipulating real physical wheels supported some angular-momentum reasoning; sensorimotor correlate was task specific. | Meaningful interaction can help, but a slider or animation is not equivalent to physical experience. |
| Mazza et al. 2016 | Sleep between paired-associate sessions improved relearning/retention in a narrow language-pair task. | Value later recall; do not infer a required sleep schedule for courses. |
| Pan & Rickard 2017 | Term–definition retrieval beat restudy for practiced responses but showed weak reversed-response transfer. | Independently test the reasoning and form the course outcome actually needs. |
| Sana et al. 2013 | Simulated-lecture laptop multitasking and nearby distraction lowered immediate test scores. | Avoid unrelated interface streams and motion while teaching. |
| Wiklund-Hörnqvist et al. 2021 | Retrieval with feedback improved week-later word-pair recall with hippocampal associations. | Supports delayed retrieval rationale, not a neural mastery metric. |
| Wing et al. 2013 | Retrieval improved next-day paired-associate recall and related fMRI measures. | Require an attempt before showing answers; do not generalize neural signals to course transfer. |
| Zou et al. 2025 | Spacing-related re-encoding signal tracked later image recognition in eight intensively scanned adults. | Revisit knowledge and support recognition/recovery; it cannot set LecturePilot's optimal interval. |
