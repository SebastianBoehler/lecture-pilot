# Learning research discovery bibliography

Collected 25 September 2026 as a bounded literature-discovery pass for
LecturePilot. This is a reading list, not a systematic review or an analysis of
the findings. Links point to publisher records, DOI pages, PubMed, or author /
institution repositories. “Open” means a public full text was located; it does
not mean every listed version is the version of record.

Paper-by-paper access and content notes are in
[2026-09-25-paper-content-and-access-notes.md](2026-09-25-paper-content-and-access-notes.md).

## 1. Learning, retrieval, spacing, and self-regulation

- **Dunlosky, Rawson, Marsh, Nathan & Willingham (2013).** “Improving Students’
  Learning With Effective Learning Techniques.” *Psychological Science in the
  Public Interest*, 14(1), 4–58. [DOI and article page](https://doi.org/10.1177/1529100612453266) ·
  [public full text](https://www.psychologicalscience.org/publications/journals/pspi/learning-techniques.html).
- **Karpicke & Blunt (2011).** “Retrieval Practice Produces More Learning than
  Elaborative Studying with Concept Mapping.” *Science*, 331(6018), 772–775.
  [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1199327) ·
  [author-hosted copy](https://ctl.yale.edu/sites/default/files/files/KarpickeBlunt2011.pdf).
- **Adesope, Trevisan & Sundararajan (2017).** “Rethinking the Use of Tests: A
  Meta-Analysis of Practice Testing.” *Review of Educational Research*, 87(3),
  659–701. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.3102/0034654316689306) ·
  [WSU record](https://rex.libraries.wsu.edu/esploro/outputs/journalArticle/Rethinking-the-Use-of-Tests-A/99900601154801842).
- **Cepeda et al. (2006).** “Distributed Practice in Verbal Recall Tasks: A
  Review and Quantitative Synthesis.” *Psychological Bulletin*, 132(3),
  354–380. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1037/0033-2909.132.3.354).
- **Agarwal, Nunes & Blunt (2021).** “Retrieval Practice Consistently Benefits
  Student Learning: A Systematic Review of Applied Research in Schools and
  Classrooms.” *Educational Psychology Review*, 33, 1409–1453.
  [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-021-09595-9).
- **Panadero (2017).** “A Review of Self-Regulated Learning: Six Models and
  Four Directions for Research.” *Frontiers in Psychology*, 8, 422.
  [Open full text](https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2017.00422).
- **Jansen, van Leeuwen, Janssen, Jak & Kester (2019).** “Self-regulated
  learning partially mediates the effect of self-regulated learning
  interventions on achievement in higher education: A meta-analysis.”
  *Educational Research Review*, 28, 100292.
  [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.edurev.2019.100292).
- **Nückles et al. (2020).** “The Self-Regulation-View in Writing-to-Learn:
  Using Journal Writing to Optimize Cognitive Load in Self-Regulated Learning.”
  *Educational Psychology Review*, 32, 1089–1126.
  [Open full text](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-020-09541-1).
- **Toppino, LaVan & Iaconelli (2018).** “Metacognitive control in
  self-regulated learning: Conditions affecting the choice of restudying versus
  retrieval practice.” *Memory & Cognition*, 46, 1164–1177.
  [DOI](https://doi.org/10.3758/s13421-018-0828-2) · IU EBSCO surfaced a
  peer-reviewed record under the “Available in Library Collection” filter;
  PDF entitlement not checked.
- **Martins van Jaarsveld et al. (2025).** “Enhancing Goal Attainment in Higher
  Education with a Scripted Conversational Agent: Effects of Monitoring and
  Reflection Support in Digital Learning.” *Computers & Education*, 239,
  105441. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2025.105441) · IU EBSCO
  surfaced a peer-reviewed record in the library-collection search; PDF
  entitlement not checked.
- **Wagner et al. (2024).** “The more, the better? Learning with feedback and
  instruction.” *Learning and Instruction*, 89, 101844. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2023.101844) ·
  [Tübingen-hosted author manuscript](https://publikationen.uni-tuebingen.de/xmlui/bitstream/handle/10900/153156/SalomeWagner_Dissertation_Beyond-the-Screen.pdf?isAllowed=n&sequence=2).
- **Atkinson, Renkl & Merrill (2003).** “Transitioning from Studying Examples
  to Solving Problems: Effects of Self-Explanation Prompts and Fading Worked-Out
  Steps.” *Journal of Educational Psychology*, 95(4), 774–783.
  [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1037/0022-0663.95.4.774).

## 2. AI and learning

These are direct candidate studies or syntheses. Several were already recorded
in [the 6 September AI evidence update](2026-09-06-ai-learning-and-evaluation-update.md);
repeated entries are cross-references rather than new evaluations.

- **Bastani et al. (2025).** “Generative AI without guardrails can harm
  learning: Evidence from high school mathematics.” *PNAS*, 122(26),
  e2422633122. [Open article](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2422633122) ·
  [study repository](https://github.com/obastani/GenAICanHarmLearning).
- **Kestin et al. (2025).** “AI tutoring outperforms in-class active learning:
  an RCT introducing a novel research-based design in an authentic educational
  setting.” *Scientific Reports*, 15, 17458.
  [Open article](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-97652-6).
- **Wang et al. (2024).** “Tutor CoPilot: A Human-AI Approach for Scaling
  Real-Time Expertise.” [Open preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.03017).
- **Deng et al. (2025).** “Does ChatGPT enhance student learning? A systematic
  review and meta-analysis of experimental studies.” *Computers & Education*,
  227, 105224. [DOI and publisher page](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2024.105224) ·
  publisher full-text access may depend on library entitlement.
- **“ChatGPT as a cognitive crutch: Evidence from a randomized controlled trial
  on knowledge retention” (2025).** *Social Sciences & Humanities Open*, 12,
  102287. [Open article](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ssaho.2025.102287).
- **“The impact of GenAI on learning outcomes: A systematic review and
  meta-analysis of experimental studies” (2025).** *Educational Research
  Review*. [DOI and publisher page](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.edurev.2025.100741).
- **LearnLM Team / Google & Eedi (2025).** “AI tutoring can safely and
  effectively support students: An exploratory RCT in UK classrooms.”
  [Open preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.23633).

  Attention Cueing in Instructional Animations: Guidelines for Research and

- **de Koning, Tabbers, Rikers & Paas (2010).** “Attention guidance in learning
  from a complex animation: Seeing is understanding?” *Learning and
  Instruction*, 20(2), 111–122. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2009.02.010).
- **Ainsworth (2006).** “DeFT: A conceptual framework for considering learning
  with multiple representations.” *Learning and Instruction*, 16(3), 183–198.
  [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2006.03.001).
- **Berney & Bétrancourt (2016).** “Does animation enhance learning? A
  meta-analysis.” *Computers & Education*, 101, 150–167.
  [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2016.06.005).
- **Ploetzner et al. (2021).** “When learning from animations is more successful
  than learning from static pictures: learning the specifics of change.”
  *Instructional Science*. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-021-09541-w).

  [the display-learning evidence note](2026-09-06-display-media-learning-evidence.md).
- **Hegarty (2004).** “Dynamic visualizations and learning: Getting to the

  [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2004.06.007).
- **Paas & van Merriënboer (2020).** “Cognitive-Load Theory: Methods to Manage

  [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/0963721420922183).
- **Porta Simó et al. (2015).** “Effects of Applying the Site Map Principle in
  an Online Learning Environment in Higher Education.” *International Journal

  [Open full text](https://online-journals.org/index.php/i-jet/article/download/4620/3471).
- **Karich, Burns & Maki (2014).** “Updated Meta-Analysis of Learner Control

  392–410. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.3102/0034654314526064).
- **Stoesz, Niknam & Sutton (2020).** “Defining the Visual Complexity of
  Learning Management Systems Using Image Metrics and Subjective Ratings.”

  [Open full text](https://files.eric.ed.gov/fulltext/EJ1284134.pdf).
- **Shapiro (2008).** “Hypermedia Design as Learner Scaffolding.” *Educational

  [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-007-9063-4).
- **Russ et al. (2026).** “Combining Non-Interactive Teaching and Drawing
  Fosters Conceptual Knowledge but Not Monitoring Accuracy From Guided Inquiry

  [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000971) · author-deposited paper located
  in [Heike Russ’s Tübingen dissertation](https://publikationen.uni-tuebingen.de/xmlui/bitstream/handle/10900/169303/Russ_Heike_2025_Dissertation.pdf?isAllowed=y&sequence=1).

## 4. Neuroscience and memory mechanisms

The existing [neuroscience evidence note](2026-09-06-neuroscience-learning-evidence.md)
contains ten inspected original studies. These additional full-text candidates
extend the reading set:

- **Wiklund-Hörnqvist et al. (2021).** “Retrieval practice facilitates learning
  by strengthening processing in both the anterior and posterior hippocampus.”
  [Open author manuscript](https://www.diva-portal.org/smash/get/diva2:1484125/FULLTEXT02.pdf).
- **Payne et al. (2012).** “Memory for Semantically Related and Unrelated
  Declarative Information: The Benefit of Sleep, the Cost of Wake.”
  [Open PMC full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3310860/).
- **Landmann et al. (2016).** “Sleep Strengthens but Does Not Reorganize Memory
  Traces in a Verbal Creativity Task.”
  [Open PMC full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4763358/).
- **Schabus et al. (2018).** “The effect of daytime napping and full-night sleep
  on the consolidation of declarative and procedural information.”
  [PubMed / PMC full-text route](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29271015/).
- **Denis et al. (2026).** “Theta oscillations tag episodic memories for
  sleep-dependent consolidation.” *PLOS Biology*.
  [Open full text](https://journals.plos.org/plosbiology/article?id=10.1371/journal.pbio.3003938) ·
  [data and code](https://doi.org/10.17605/OSF.IO/TPWVB).
